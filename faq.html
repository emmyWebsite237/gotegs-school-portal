Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the things parents and students ask us most.

01How do I enrol my child at Go-Tegs?

Fill out the form on our Enrolment page. We'll review it and reach out to arrange class screening before registration is completed.

02How do I check my child's result?

Go to the Student Portal. Once inside the portal, select Results and enter the result PIN.

03How do I get a School Leaving Testimonial?

Open the Student Portal and use the School Leaving Testimonial area when the feature is activated.

04How do I order books or stationery?

Open the Store from the Student Portal and follow the order and payment instructions provided there.

05What programmes does Go-Tegs offer?

Preparatory, Kindergarten / Pre-K, Nursery, Primary, Junior Arm, Senior Arm and extracurricular opportunities.

06How do I contact the school directly?

Call 0806 652 5319 or 0810 695 8638, email gotegs622@gmail.com, use WhatsApp, or submit the enquiry form on our Contact page.
