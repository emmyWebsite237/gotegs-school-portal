Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick, direct answers to common questions about admissions, student portals, terminal exams, curriculum, and campus life.

01 How do I enrol my child at Go-Tegs International School?
You can initiate enrolment directly on our Admissions Page. Simply complete the online application, and our admissions team will schedule an informal interaction and placement assessment for your child.
02 How can my child or I check term examination results?
Results are released securely on our private Student Portal. Sign in with the student's ID and PIN, click on Results, and enter your verified scratch-card result PIN to inspect or download the official report sheet.
03 How do students access downloadable curriculum lesson notes?
Students can log in to the Student Portal and open the Lesson Notes section. Notes are organized by Class and Academic Term, allowing online reading or direct Microsoft Word (.DOCX) downloads.
04 What age groups and academic divisions are accommodated?
Go-Tegs offers education across four key academies: Early Years (Preparatory, Pre-K, Nursery), Primary School (Grades 1 to 6), Junior Secondary (JSS 1 to 3), and Senior Secondary (SSS 1 to 3) with specialized Science, Arts, and Commercial tracks.
05 Where is the campus located, and how can we schedule a visit?
Our campus is located at 15, Ayesanmi Street, GloryLand Estate, Powerline Bus Stop, Isheri Olofin, Alimosho, Lagos. You can reach out through our Contact Page or call 0806 652 5319 to arrange a guided campus walkthrough.
06 How does the CBT practice testing system work?
The Student Portal features an interactive Computer-Based Testing (CBT) engine. Students can take timed practice quizzes across various subjects to prepare for WAEC, NECO, BECE, and JAMB UTME exams.
